What to Expect

A friendly guide for our Dutch guests attending their first Bengali wedding

🍛

A Feast of Bengali Flavours

Bengali celebrations centre around bountiful hospitality. You will enjoy a multi-course traditional banquet featuring fragrant Kacchi Biryani (tender meat slow-cooked with basmati rice and saffron potatoes), succulent kebabs, mild aromatic curries, and Bengal's legendary handmade sweets: Roshogolla, Sandesh, and Mishti Doi. Dishes are warmly spiced with cinnamon, cardamom, and ghee rather than fiery heat.

🥻

Festive Colours & Attire

Guests are warmly invited to dress in joyful, vibrant colours! If you would like to wear traditional attire—a saree for ladies or an embroidered panjabi for gentlemen—our family will gladly help you choose one in Dhaka. Alternatively, festive Western wear (a smart suit, blazer, or cocktail dress) is equally wonderful. Comfort and celebration come first!

🌸

Warm Traditions & Hospitality

In Bengali culture, guests are welcomed like cherished family (Atithi Devo Bhava). You will be greeted with floral garlands and sweets upon arrival. There are no stiff formalities—our bilingual family members and hosts will be with you throughout the evening to introduce customs, guide you through the dishes, and make sure you feel right at home.
